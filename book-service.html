Client Confidentiality & Privacy

At Axion Designs, we hold the highest standards of architectural confidentiality. Any project information, property drawings, and contact details you provide are processed strictly for assessing your project requirements and scheduling your consultation.

Data Protection Principles:

  • We never sell, rent, or distribute client contact information to third-party telemarketers.
  • Submissions are encrypted and stored in secure private studio archives.
  • You may request complete erasure of your consultation request at any time by contacting our studio desk.

For inquiries regarding our design records, please write to our concierge at concierge@axiondesigns.com.
